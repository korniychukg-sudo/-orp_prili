# Deep Blue v2.1 — Learn Edition (design spec)

Date: 2026-07-28
App: Deep Blue (`com.deepbluedive.app`), delivered v2 → content rework in place in
`for_human_review_apps/Deep Blue`
Goal: the app looks and plays well but is still thin on *substance* — it shows
creatures and depths without ever teaching how the ocean works. This edition adds
a Learn tab with twenty illustrated guides, a quiz, a tripled glossary, and
conservation context on every animal, so the app reads as a genuinely useful
reference rather than a pretty list. Ships as **v1.0 (build 1)** per the
versioning rule.

## Problem

v2 delivered the expedition loop (vessels, descent, ranks, daily objectives). But
the *knowledge* layer is shallow: 10 glossary terms, 10 rotating facts, three
facts per creature, and **no explanatory content at all** — nothing tells the user
why the deep is dark, what causes tides, how animals survive the pressure, or what
threatens the deep sea. Master's brief: more content, guides, information about
animals and tides; make it fuller and higher quality, not a throwaway reference.

## Decisions (confirmed with Master in chat)

1. Target app: **Deep Blue** (Master chose it over Inner Tides).
2. Shape: **a new "Learn" tab — the full package** — ~20 illustrated guides, a
   quiz, and an expanded glossary, plus deeper creature entries.
3. Tides are covered **as guides with a static diagram, not an interactive
   simulator** — see §3 for the reasoning.
4. iPad support already shipped in v2; nothing further needed there.
5. Unchanged constraints: iOS 15.6+, custom UI only (no SF Symbols / emoji /
   system components), forced dark, fully offline, no notifications, no
   permissions, manual signing, WebView launch gate untouched, **version 1.0
   (build 1)**, app size ≥ 18 MB and < 99 MB.

---

## 1. The Learn tab

A fifth tab in the existing custom tab bar (`Dive · Journal · Zones · Learn ·
More`). Five tabs fit the current `HStack` bar; icon is a new custom Canvas glyph
(an open book over a wave), consistent with the existing glyph set.

The tab is a hub with three sections and a progress header:

```
[ header: "Learn" banner + "7 of 20 guides read" ring ]
[ Guides    — 4 category groups, 20 cards ]
[ Quiz      — entry card: best score, "10 questions" ]
[ Glossary  — entry row: 32 terms ]
```

Reading a guide marks it read (persisted) and awards **+6 XP the first time only**
(a new `XPAward.guide = 6`); re-reading awards nothing.

## 2. Twenty guides

Each guide has: an id, a category, a title, a one-line teaser, a banner image, 3–5
paragraphs of body text, an optional list of related creature ids, and an optional
list of related glossary terms. Related creatures render as tappable thumbnails
that open the existing `CreatureDetailView`; related terms open the glossary entry.

**How the ocean works (6)**
1. `tides` — What Makes the Tides: the Moon's pull, the Sun's smaller pull, why
   there are two bulges, spring vs neap tides. Includes the static diagram (§3).
2. `currents` — The Great Currents: wind-driven surface gyres, the Gulf Stream,
   why currents steer climate.
3. `conveyor` — The Ocean Conveyor: thermohaline circulation, cold salty water
   sinking at the poles, the thousand-year loop.
4. `pressure` — Why the Pressure Crushes: one atmosphere per 10 m, what that does
   to gas spaces, why deep animals have none.
5. `light` — Where the Light Goes: absorption by wavelength, why red vanishes
   first and blue last, why the deep is black.
6. `waves` — How Water Actually Moves: wind waves vs swell, orbital motion,
   why water does not travel with a wave.

**Life in the dark (5)**
7. `biolum` — Living Light: the luciferin/luciferase reaction, and the four uses —
   luring, startling, counter-illumination, signalling.
8. `migration` — The Great Migration: diel vertical migration, the largest daily
   animal movement on Earth, why it happens.
9. `snow` — Marine Snow: what it is made of, how slowly it falls, why it is the
   deep's entire food supply.
10. `whalefall` — When a Whale Falls: the three-stage succession over decades —
    scavengers, enrichment opportunists, then the sulphophilic stage.
11. `chemo` — Life Without Sun: chemosynthesis at hydrothermal vents, bacteria
    feeding on hydrogen sulphide, whole ecosystems with no sunlight.

**Built for the deep (4)**
12. `adaptpressure` — Built for Pressure: no swim bladders, watery flesh, and the
    molecule (TMAO) that keeps proteins working — plus why it sets a depth limit.
13. `gigantism` — Giants of the Deep: deep-sea gigantism, cold and oxygen as
    explanations, the giant isopod and colossal squid as cases.
14. `eyes` — Eyes in the Dark: tubular eyes, upward gaze, reflective layers, and
    the red-light hunters that see what their prey cannot.
15. `hunting` — Hunting Where Food Is Rare: lures, huge mouths, distensible
    stomachs, slow metabolism, and long waits between meals.

**The ocean and us (5)**
16. `unmapped` — The Unmapped Ocean: how little of the seafloor is mapped in
    detail, how sonar mapping works, why it matters.
17. `humans` — How Deep People Have Gone: Trieste in 1960, the modern full-ocean
    submersibles, and what a dive to the trench actually involves.
18. `plastic` — Plastic in the Trench: how debris reaches the deepest points, what
    has been found there, microplastics in hadal animals.
19. `warming` — A Warming, Souring Sea: heat uptake, acidification chemistry, what
    it does to shells and reefs.
20. `protect` — Protecting the Deep: marine protected areas, the deep-sea mining
    debate, and why slow-growing deep life recovers poorly.

All copy is original, written in the app's existing plain, warm register — the
same voice as the current creature facts. English (US).

## 3. Tides: guides, not a second simulator

Master asked specifically for tide content. The portfolio already contains
**Inner Tides**, whose centrepiece is an interactive "Tide Machine" where the user
drags the Moon and Sun around a living Earth.

Rebuilding that interaction inside Deep Blue would make two of Master's own apps
visibly similar in their standout mechanic — precisely the pattern App Store
guideline 4.3(a) targets, and the risk is worse between apps from the same
account. Deep Blue therefore covers tides through:

- guide 1 (`tides`) with a **static, non-interactive diagram** rendered as a
  generated PNG: Earth with its two tidal bulges, the Moon, and a spring/neap
  comparison strip;
- a short tide-zone note inside the Sunlight Zone material.

The topic is fully covered; the mechanic is not duplicated.

## 4. Quiz

- A pool of **45 questions: 30 generated from existing data** (at what depth does X
  live, which zone is Y in, which of these is larger, which of these makes its own
  light) and **15 hand-written from the guides** (what causes spring tides, what
  marine snow is made of).
- A round is 10 questions: the pool is shuffled with a seeded generator whose seed
  is the **round counter** (a persisted `quizRounds: Int`, incremented after each
  finished round), so every round differs, a given round is stable if the view
  redraws mid-round, and no run depends on `Math.random`-style nondeterminism.
  Each question is multiple choice with four options, one correct.
- Correct answer: +2 XP (restores `XPAward.quiz`, which v2 removed as unused) and
  a one-line explanation. Wrong: the correct answer is shown with the same
  explanation — the quiz teaches rather than punishes.
- Best score is persisted and shown on the quiz card.

## 5. Deeper creature entries

Each of the 50 creatures gains two fields:

- `conservation: ConservationStatus` — an enum with cases `leastConcern`,
  `nearThreatened`, `vulnerable`, `endangered`, `dataDeficient`, each with a
  display name and colour. Rendered as a pill in `CreatureDetailView`.
  Species with no meaningful assessment (most deep-sea invertebrates) get
  `dataDeficient`, which is itself informative and honest.
- `whyItMatters: String` — one sentence on the animal's role or significance
  (what eats it, what it eats, what it tells scientists).

Glossary grows 10 → 32 terms; rotating facts grow 10 → 30.

## 6. Awards

Six new awards (18 → 24):

| id | Title | Requirement |
|---|---|---|
| `learn_first` | First Lesson | Read one guide |
| `learn_half` | Well Read | Read 10 guides |
| `learn_all` | Ocean Scholar | Read all 20 guides |
| `quiz_first` | Quick Study | Finish a quiz round |
| `quiz_perfect` | Perfect Round | Answer all 10 correctly |
| `glossary_all` | Word by Word | Open every glossary term |

## 7. Architecture & files

New:
- `Deep Blue/GuideData.swift` — `Guide`, `GuideCategory`, the 20 guides.
- `Deep Blue/QuizEngine.swift` — `QuizQuestion`, generation from data + authored
  questions, deterministic round selection. Pure logic.
- `Deep Blue/LearnView.swift` — the Learn hub.
- `Deep Blue/GuideDetailView.swift` — a single guide, with related creatures/terms.
- `Deep Blue/QuizView.swift` — the quiz round and result screen.

Extended:
- `OceanModels.swift` — `ConservationStatus`; `Creature.conservation`,
  `Creature.whyItMatters`.
- `OceanData.swift` — conservation + why-it-matters on all 50 creatures, glossary
  10 → 32, facts 10 → 30, +6 awards.
- `DeepStore.swift` — `guidesRead: Set<String>`, `termsOpened: Set<String>`,
  `quizBest: Int`, `quizRounds: Int`; all optional-decoded so **v2 and v1 saves
  keep loading**.
- `RootView.swift` — fifth tab.
- `DeepIcons.swift` — the Learn glyph.
- `CreatureDetailView.swift` — conservation pill + why-it-matters line.
- `art_src/generate_art.py` — 20 guide banners, the tide diagram, the Learn banner.

Guides and quiz questions live in their own files rather than swelling
`OceanData.swift` (already 676 lines); each new file has one clear job.

## 8. Art

22 new opaque PNGs: 20 guide banners (abstract, diagrammatic, in the existing
muted deep-sea palette), the spring/neap tide diagram, and the Learn tab banner.
Generated by extending the existing pure-PIL `generate_art.py`. Expected app size
~53 MB (≥ 18 MB, < 99 MB).
