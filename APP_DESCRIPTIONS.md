# App Descriptions

Мастер-список выпущенных приложений (имя + 1 строка описания).

| App Name | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Plaint Aid | Diagnose common houseplant problems by answering a couple of quick questions, then get clear treatment and prevention steps — fully offline. |
| Fridgeful | Select the ingredients you already have at home and instantly see which recipes you can cook, ranked by match — with unique cover illustrations for every recipe, world-cuisine browsing, curated collections, a guided cook mode, and a shopping list for what's missing. Fully offline. |
| Pocket First Aid | Calm, illustrated, step-by-step guidance for 32 everyday emergencies with severity badges, a full-screen guided mode, and aftercare advice — plus a haptic CPR rhythm coach, a guide of the day, kit checklists that explain every item, a 42-question quiz, a first aid Myth Busters game, and emergency numbers for 18 regions. No account, no permissions, fully offline. |
| Photo Sweep | Clean up your photo library one swipe at a time: swipe right to keep, left to bin, then delete in one confirmed batch. Smart stacks surface screenshots, huge videos, duplicates and month-old piles; a monthly challenge, 18 unlockable award badges and illustrated storage tips keep the habit going. Fully illustrated, haptic, all on-device. |
| Daily Brain | A calm five-minute daily brain workout: a balanced set of five mini-games picked each day from 19 custom games across memory, numbers, focus and reflex, with a skill radar profile, streaks, XP levels, session history and personal bests — fully offline with generated art for every game. |
| Night Sky Tales | An offline illustrated guide to 24 constellations: an auto-detected seasonal sky map you can pan, each constellation's real star pattern drawn as a live twinkling diagram, a retold myth, how-to-find tips and sky facts, plus thematic myth collections, a daily tale, a spotted-and-favourites journal with progress badges — richly generated star art throughout, fully offline. |
| Birds Next Door | An illustrated offline field guide to 40 backyard birds paired with a personal life list: log every sighting with its date, place, count and notes, watch per-group progress fill in, and earn 16 milestone badges. Includes a bird-of-the-day, seasonal "in season now" highlights, search with color/size/group filters, lookalike comparisons, a ten-question ID quiz, 8 birding tip guides and a 24-term glossary — every species portrait individually generated, fully offline. |
| Knotboard | Learn 28 knots by feel: drag your finger across a realistic three-strand hemp rope and it ties itself stitch by stitch with correct over-and-under crossings — scrub back and it unties. Every knot is presented museum-style, mounted on a wooden knot board with an engraved brass name plate; learned knots join your own Knot Board collection wall, and an "I need to…" finder picks the right knot for the job (tie to a post, join ropes, make a loop…) with a reason for each. Seven categories, numbered steps that light up as you tie, real history, fun facts, a name-the-knot quiz, rope tips, glossary, streaks, XP and 16 badges — fully offline. |
| Murmora | Build living ambient soundscapes by layering 30 hand-crafted sounds — rain, campfire, ocean, cicadas, distant wolves, purring cat, singing bowl and more — and watch your mix come alive: the scene fills with falling rain, rising embers, rolling waves, blinking fireflies and drifting stars that respond to what you play. Enter full-screen Immerse mode for a glowing bedside clock over the living scene, blend everything at once, save your own scenes, or start from 16 curated presets like Rainy Cabin, Snowy Cabin and Temple Calm. Drift off with a fade-out sleep timer or lock in with focus sessions, and grow your listening level, most-played sounds and badges. Every sound is generated on device — no streaming, no account, no permissions, fully offline. |
| Doodli | Learn to draw with your finger on a guided sketchbook journey: a winding path through 33 step-by-step doodle lessons in 6 chapters, opened by warm-up drills with 1-3 star accuracy scoring and rewarded with collectible chapter stickers. Lessons trace over soft animated guides that draw themselves stroke by stroke with a moving pen dot; finished works land on a tilted, washi-taped gallery wall and can be replayed as living drawings. Streaks, XP levels, 14 badges, confetti and haptics throughout, plus a free canvas — hand-made paper-grain look, fully offline. |
| Paper Garden | A living paper-craft self-care companion: real-life mini-rituals — a glass of water, a short walk, an hour off the phone — water a diorama that reacts to the real world: dawn, dusk and night tint the scene, fireflies drift at twilight, seasonal petals and snow float by, and layers shift under your finger. A built-in breathing coach and gentle ritual timers do the heavy lifting; a journal tab keeps your moods, one-line notes and a month heat map of tended days. 20 seasonal plant species, 14 paper creatures, 18 badges, and a pettable paper fox who nudges you kindly. Miss a few days and the garden just sleeps — one small ritual wakes it. Fully offline. |
| Mellowbend | Stretch along with Buddy — a friendly animated companion who does every stretch with you in real time, and becomes a real friend as you practice. Every session grows your friendship: Buddy unlocks new skins and accessories (dress them in a beanie, scarf or flower crown), and the cozy nook you share fills with keepsakes — a growing plant, string lights, even a sleeping cat — under a window that follows the real time of day. Follow 8 gentle routines or three 7-day journeys like Gentle Week and Desk Rescue, build your own routines stretch by stretch, and watch soft coaching cues, countdown rings and confetti keep you moving. A calendar heatmap, body balance bars, streaks and 12 badges track your care. No equipment, no account, no internet — just a few soft minutes a day with a friend. |
| Kilnfolk | A pocket pottery apprenticeship: shape real pots with one finger on a spinning wheel — push the wall in or out, sponge it smooth, rib it straight, lift it taller — then take on the 12 classic forms of pottery history (Tea Bowl, Moon Jar, Amphora, Meiping…) against a ghost silhouette with live accuracy scoring and 1-3 stars. Glaze with dips, bands and speckles, fire in a kiln whose reveal transforms the colors (with confetti, XP tallies and the occasional crackle surprise), and climb 12 potter ranks that unlock new glazes and clays. A form-of-the-day streak, 17 awards, a wooden cabinet gallery with real-size labels, and an illustrated potter's lore shelf round out the studio — fully offline. |
| Stitch Meadow | A calm corner for cross-stitch: fill charming illustrated charts one stitch at a time. Pick a floss shade, tap the matching cells and each cross pulls tight with a soft snap and a little thread-pull animation; tap a whole strand in at once, undo freely, and pinch the beech hoop to zoom in on tiny details — no counting, no mistakes, all forgiving. 28 hand-charted patterns across 7 collections (Garden Blooms, Cottage Corner, Night Sky, Little Critters, Sweet Treats, Four Seasons, Sea & Shore), each stitched from a real floss box of 20 collectible shades rendered with dimensional legs and sheen on aida cloth. Finish a piece and it's framed in wood and hung on your own gallery wall, tilted on its little nail — tap any frame to admire it full-size and replay the whole thing stitching itself back in. A daily pattern, maker levels, day streaks, 15 badges, a thread box and per-collection progress keep the meadow growing. Fully offline — no account, no permissions, no internet. |
| Little Orrery | A living Solar System that knows your sky and knows you. The animated orrery now teems with life: an asteroid-belt dust ring, a tappable long-period comet whose tail always streams away from the Sun, glowing orbit trails, occasional shooting stars and haptic feedback throughout. The Tonight tab answers a real question every evening — which planets are visible right now (evening west / morning east / up all night / hidden in the glare), under tonight's Moon phase. Tell the app your weight and birth year and every world becomes personal: your weight there, your age in local years, how high you could jump, and how long the trip would take by airliner, spacecraft or light. Board the cinematic Grand Tour — a 20-stop narrated voyage from the Sun to Eris with a counting distance odometer and confetti finale — and climb 8 explorer ranks by earning XP from visits, quizzes, notes and touring, unlocking 22 badges along the way. Plus everything from v1: 20 procedurally-painted worlds with full profiles, rotating globes, orbit diagrams, size comparisons, a daily object, a ten-question Sky Quiz and a field-note journal. No account, no internet, fully offline. |
| Puzzle Porch | Zen jigsaw puzzles from 18 original hand-made illustrations, tended on a cozy covered porch that grows with you. A real interlocking jigsaw engine slices each illustration at runtime into pieces with complementary tab-and-blank edges (guaranteed to fit); drag pieces from an adaptive tray onto the board and they gently snap home, with an optional faint reference image, golden edge-piece hints, and haptic taps. Four piece counts (Cozy 9 · Calm 16 · Serene 25 · Deep 36). Finished scenes "come alive" with a per-theme ambient overlay (ridges, ponds/koi, starry nights, groves, blossoms, harbors, dunes, meadows, mirror lakes) and hang, framed and quietly animated, in a Porch Gallery. The home screen is a living porch: a hand-drawn scene that shifts with the real time of day (lanterns light at dusk, fireflies at night) and fills in with framed finishes, a plant, a mug, a dozing cat and more as you progress. Scenes are grouped into four themed Collections; a date-based Puzzle of the Day gives a reason to return; a Journal tracks finish streaks (with a five-week calendar), pieces placed and collection progress; and 18 hand-drawn Awards celebrate milestones. Auto-saved progress, no timers or scores. iOS 15.6+, custom UI, fully offline, no notifications. |
| Fossilward | Become the curator of your own natural-history museum. Brush and scrape rock away with your finger until a buried bone appears — dig carefully and you earn a Pristine specimen, rush it with a heavy pick and you'll chip the fragile edges — then drag every freed bone onto a mount to rebuild the whole skeleton, from tiny trilobites to towering Tyrannosaurus. Uncover rare curiosities in the matrix (amber with an insect, an ammonite pearl, a fossil footprint) for your Curio Cabinet, arrange your finished skeletons in spotlit museum halls with brass placards, and bring any creature back to life in a breathing, day-to-night diorama of its ancient world. Grow your Prestige through five museum tiers, chase a daily expedition and weekly goals, and explore 18 creatures across 6 illustrated dig sites with paleoart, field notes, a fossil quiz, field guides and 30 badges. Every skeleton and living creature is drawn from parametric art; richly illustrated, haptic, fully offline — no account, no internet. |
| Belowlight | A continuous dive from the sunlit surface to the floor of the Mariana Trench, with a craft to carry you there and a Learn section to make sense of what you're seeing. Choose from five vessels — a snorkel mask, a scuba rig, a bathysphere, a deep submersible, or a full-ocean submersible — each rated to its own real depth limit, then watch it sink on its own in a cinematic descent where the light fades and, past 1,000 metres, only your lamp's cone remains as creatures drift into the glow. A sonar ping sweeps the dark for anything nearby, a new expedition objective waits every day, and diving well earns XP toward eight diver ranks, from Snorkeler to Master of the Abyss, that unlock the deeper craft. Fifty real sea creatures wait at their true depths with facts about how they live and, now, a conservation status and a note on why each one matters, spread across six illustrated habitats — coral reef, kelp forest, whale fall, hydrothermal vents, brine pool and trench floor. Twenty illustrated guides explain how the ocean actually works — tides, currents, the deep conveyor, pressure, light, waves, bioluminescence, the nightly migration of the whole water column, marine snow, whale falls, vent life, adaptation, exploration, plastic, warming and protection — backed by a 52-term glossary and a ten-question quiz that draws fresh questions each round and explains every answer. Enter your height and weight and depth becomes personal: how many of you stacked head to toe it would take to reach the bottom, and how your size compares to a giant squid or a blue whale. Keep a field journal, study zone guides and landmarks, and earn awards along the way — no account, no internet, fully offline, built for iPhone and iPad. |
| Moonpull | A living tide companion for iPhone and iPad. The Today tab opens on an animated shore that rises and falls with the modelled tide in real time — the sky follows the hour, the Moon shows its true phase, and boats, birds and stars drift by. Plan around daylight-aware Tide Windows (tidepooling, low-tide walks, golden hour, surf, fishing), drag the Moon and Sun around a living Earth in the Tide Machine, and tour 20 real coasts with live tide clocks. The Discover hub holds a 31-species Shore Life guide with a draggable Zone Explorer (pull the tide up a living shore and watch each zone flood), 7 illustrated field guides with saved checklists, 18 lessons, a 20-term glossary and a daily quiz. Tide Log journal, ranks, streaks and 30 badges; 76 generated illustrations; an educational model, fully offline, not for navigation. |
| Gears Inside | A living workshop for everyday mechanisms. Crank 16 cutaway machines with your finger — zipper, pin lock, music box, planetary gears, clock escapement, four-stroke engine and more — pull each apart into an exploded view, tap labelled parts and walk guided steps. The home is a live bench scene whose window follows the real clock while the Mechanism of the Day runs on an easel; a Test Bench lets you mount real meshing gears on a pegboard (correct ratios, jams and all) through 12 challenges; mastered mechanisms become live miniature models on your Model Hall wall; and a Repair Corner teaches 10 real fixes — stuck zippers, sticky locks, skipping chains. Field guides, glossary, quiz, daily goals, ranks, 28 awards. Fully offline, iPhone + iPad. |
| Hive Warm | Tend a living honey-bee colony through the turning year. Each day hands you three tasks' worth of daylight: open the hive and read the frames one by one — hex-cell comb drawn live, honey band up top, brood nest below, the queen hiding on one frame for you to spot — then feed syrup, drop a pollen patty, treat for varroa, stack another box, or spin the extractor (five kilograms always stay with the bees). The colony simulates on under real seasonal pressure: bloom curves and weather set the nectar flow, the queen's lay responds to pollen and space, mites multiply quietly, crowding builds toward a swarm you can catch off the branch or watch fly, wasps test the door in autumn, mice move in over winter, and an unwrapped hive bleeds heat on bitter nights. Without a recent inspection the dashboard only estimates — open the hive to truly know. Sell jars at the market (autumn fairs pay best), stock the supply shed, and carry the colony to spring: the Keeper's Journal tracks winters survived, swarms caught, and twelve badges, while a 20-chapter illustrated Bee Almanac teaches the real craft behind every mechanic, from the waggle dance to the winter cluster. The apiary itself is alive: bees orbit the hive in fair weather and vanish in rain, snow drifts past a wrapped winter hive, a tap on the boxes tells you how the hum sounds today, and a forecast chip lets you plan tomorrow's work. A wellbeing dial distills the whole colony into one honest number with the weakest link named, every queen carries her own name from Willow to Yarrow, harvests end in a rain of golden drops, and each finished season mails a keepsake postcard into the journal beside the turning year-wheel. Fully offline — no account, no permissions, everything on device. |
| Paper Folds | An interactive origami studio: drag glowing corners and the paper folds for real — a live fold engine creases, flips and turns 20 models across 5 collections (animal faces, boats, blooms, everyday things), each finished piece gaining hand-drawn details and a place on your wooden gallery shelf with auto-play replays. Unlock 25 washi paper designs as you rank up through 8 folder ranks, keep streaks, earn 18 badges, read 8 illustrated guides on origami history and science, master a 28-term glossary and a fresh 10-question quiz each round. Confetti, haptics, generated art throughout — no account, no internet, fully offline. |
